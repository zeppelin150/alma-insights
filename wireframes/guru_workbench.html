Billing Deduction Process
Last verified: Feb 14, 2026 · Owner: CX Ops
Billing & Payments

1. Overview

This card outlines the standard process for handling provider billing deductions. CXAs should follow these steps when a provider contacts us regarding unexpected deductions from their payout.

2. Resolution Steps

  1. Step 1: Verify the provider's identity and pull up their account in the Provider Portal.
  2. Step 2: Navigate to Billing > Deduction History to review recent deductions.
  3. Step 3: Confirm the deduction matches the scheduled rate table adjustment.
Step 3a (NEW): Check the 'Source' column for 'AUTO-v2.4'. If present, this deduction was auto-applied by the billing engine. Follow the auto-deduction appeal pathway in Section 4b below instead of standard escalation.
  1. Step 4: If the deduction is correct, walk the provider through the rate table changes.
  1. Step 5: Escalation
    If unresolved, escalate to Billing Support L2 via the standard escalation form. Expected response time: 48 hours.
    If Source = AUTO-v2.4, escalate to the Billing Automation Team (not L2). They have direct override access with no 48hr hold. For all other deductions, use the standard L2 escalation.

3. Common Scenarios

Scenario 1: Provider states deduction was not communicated. Direct them to the rate change notification email sent on the 1st of each month.

Scenario 2: Deduction amount differs from published rates. Verify the effective date — rate changes may have a 30-day lag.

Scenario 4b (NEW): Provider disputes auto-deduction amount
89 tickets in the last 30 days match this pattern. Provider calls stating deduction amount doesn't match expected payout. Root cause: v2.4 applies deductions using updated rate tables that haven't been communicated to providers yet. Resolution: Confirm the deduction source is AUTO-v2.4, explain the timing gap, and offer a manual review by the Automation Team if the provider requests it.
Needs confirmation: 23 tickets suggest deductions apply before the provider's billing cycle closes. This may be a v2.4 batch processing bug rather than expected behavior. Do not include in runbook until Billing Automation Team confirms.
